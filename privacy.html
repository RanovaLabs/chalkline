Privacy Policy

Effective 6 September 2026

The short version. Chalkline has no server and no user accounts. We do not collect, receive, transmit or store any of your personal data. Your photos and job records stay on your device, and — if you subscribe to Chalkline Pro — in your own private iCloud account, which we cannot access.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Chalkline iOS app ("the app") published by Kasun Rangana M W ("we", "us"). It explains what the app does with information on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party software development kits. We do not receive your photos, job records, location or any personal information.

Crash and performance diagnostics

Chalkline stores crash and performance reports produced by iOS on your device, so you can send one to support if something goes wrong. They stay on your device and are transmitted only if you choose to share one. You can view and delete them at any time in Settings → Diagnostics.

Separately, if you have turned on Share With App Developers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may give us anonymised crash diagnostics for Chalkline. That is an Apple feature you control, not something the app collects, and it never includes your photos or job records. It does not create an account for you, does not ask for your name or email, and sends nothing to us. We have no server that could receive your data.

What the app stores on your device

Everything the app produces is written to your device's own storage:

  • Photographs you capture or import, and the small preview images generated from them
  • For each photograph: the date and time it was captured, the coordinates and street address if location is available, any note you type, the stage you assign it, and a SHA-256 fingerprint of the image
  • Job records: the name, client, and site address you enter
  • Client sign-off: the signature drawn on screen, the name typed alongside it, and the time it was saved
  • Your business details and logo, if you add them
  • A single preference recording whether you have seen the introduction screens

Deleting the app removes all of this from your device.

iCloud sync

Chalkline Pro subscribers can have their jobs and photos synchronised between their own devices using Apple's CloudKit service. This data is stored in your private iCloud database, under your Apple Account. It is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We have no access to it, cannot read it, and receive no copy of it.

Permissions the app asks for, and why

Camera
To photograph the job site. Photographs are written straight to your device. The app does not use the camera at any other time.
Photo Library
To let you add photographs you have already taken to a job, and to save a stamped copy back to your library if you choose to share one. The app reads only the specific items you select.
Location — While Using the App
To record where a photograph was taken, which is the core purpose of the app: a site log that proves where work happened. Location is read only while a job screen is open in the foreground, and never in the background. Coordinates are written onto the photograph and stored with it on your device. They are not transmitted anywhere. If you decline this permission the app continues to work, and photographs are stamped with the date and time only.

Reports you share

When you export a PDF report or share a stamped photograph, the app hands the file to the iOS share sheet and you choose where it goes — email, messages, a file, a printer. That transfer is between you and the service you pick. We are not involved and receive no copy.

Purchases

Chalkline Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and manages the subscription. We never see your payment details, billing address, or Apple Account information. Apple provides us with anonymous, aggregated sales figures that cannot identify you.

Children

Chalkline is a tool for building trades and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because we do not collect information at all.

Your rights over your data

Because your data never leaves your control, you do not need to ask us to access, correct, export or delete it:

  • Access and export — every job can be exported as a PDF from within the app at any time.
  • Correct — job details, notes, stages and sign-off can all be edited in the app.
  • Delete — delete individual photographs or whole jobs in the app. Deleting the app removes everything from the device. To remove synced copies, delete the app's data from iCloud in Settings › Apple Account › iCloud.

We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to disclose, sell or delete. We have never sold user data and have none to sell.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins handling data differently, this page will be updated and the effective date above changed before that version ships. Material changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
